> Time your moves! Complete puzzles in as least moves as possible!

In _Wait-a-sec-_ you have to solve multiple puzzles and try complete them in as least moves as possible.

The game has 10 levels, everyone getting more complex as the game goes on.

Mechanics:

You can move with WASD or the arrow keys, if you press shift you can move two tiles at once. Everytime you move the counter goes down, the blocks react to the counter in different ways, some disappear after some time, some appear in a specific time and others toggle!

Think clever and solve puzzles as efficiently as possible.

You can also undo moves by pressing Z or reset the level with Ctrl+Z
